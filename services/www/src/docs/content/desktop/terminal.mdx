---
title: "Terminal"
description: "Run persistent workspace shells in the dock with tab management and automatic reconnection."
---

The built-in `terminal` plugin adds an integrated terminal dock powered by `ghostty-web`. Terminals are scoped to the workspace directory, so your open tabs and running processes remain intact as you switch between sessions in the same project.

## Open and manage terminals

Press **Ctrl+\`** or run `/terminal` in the composer to toggle the terminal dock. If the workspace has no open terminals yet, OpenCode creates `Terminal 1` automatically when the dock opens.

| Command ID | Title | Default shortcut | Slash command |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `terminal.toggle` | Toggle terminal | `Ctrl+\`` | `/terminal` |
| `terminal.new` | New terminal | `Ctrl+Alt+T` | — |
| `terminal.close` | Close terminal | `Cmd+W` / `Ctrl+W` (inside terminal) | — |

You can place the dock either below or beside the session timeline under **Settings > Preferences > Terminal placement**. On narrow windows, open **More > Terminal** to switch to the full-screen terminal view.

## Tabs

The terminal header strip lists every shell in the current workspace directory:

- **Create**: Click the **+** button (**New terminal**) or press **Ctrl+Alt+T**. New tabs take the lowest unused number (`Terminal 1`, `Terminal 2`, …).
- **Reorder**: Drag tabs horizontally along the strip.
- **Rename**: Double-click a tab title, or right-click the tab and select **Rename**. Press **Enter** to save or **Escape** to cancel.
- **Close**: Click the tab's close button, middle-click the tab, right-click and select **Close**, or press **Cmd+W** / **Ctrl+W** while the terminal has focus.

When the last terminal tab exits (for example after typing `exit` in the shell) or closes, the dock closes automatically.

## Keyboard and mouse behavior

- **Copy and paste**: Standard clipboard copy and paste work automatically, along with **Ctrl+Shift+C** to copy the current selection.
- **Delete to line start**: On macOS, **Cmd+Backspace** sends `\x15` (`Ctrl+U`) to clear to the start of the line.
- **Open links**: Hold **Cmd**, **Ctrl**, or **Shift** and click any URL in terminal output to open it in your system browser.
- **Appearance**: The terminal automatically follows your active OpenCode light or dark theme and the monospace font configured in **Settings > Appearance**.

## Persistence and reconnection

Each terminal keeps 10,000 rows of live scrollback while mounted and persists the last 2,000 rows, cursor position, scroll offset, and dimensions across restarts.

If the connection to the OpenCode server drops, the terminal retries automatically (starting at 250 ms and backing off up to 4 seconds). If the underlying PTY process exited while disconnected, OpenCode spawns a fresh shell under the same tab title when you return.
